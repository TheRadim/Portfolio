Delivery and possible next steps

I recommend delivery as a basket choice with a calculated charge, rather than a production-service item. Product delivery eligibility is optional; coverage, timing and pricing need confirmation. Resources and support can explain the agreed offer.

Compatible extras and kit suggestions are recommendations for the new experience. They were not verified as existing features. Suggestions need reliable compatibility, kit contents, dates and prices, and must not automatically change a customer’s basket.

The next step is presenting the recommendation to Frederik and discussing my possible contribution. Visuals/UI mockups can follow if requested. Catalogue mapping and redirects remain possible later work, not a committed next deliverable. Old WordPress quick-win estimates are historical; the underlying findings remain useful for the new platform.

Sources and limits

I used the public-page review from 21 October, the October desktop/mobile design and stylesheet reviews, and the earlier June technical audit and August planning notes. The older technical records have not been reconciled against a current export. The October sitemap review reports 765 products, 94 product categories, 35 pages, 8 posts and 6 FAQ entries.

I have treated the following points carefully:

  • The June and October counts are different snapshots, not a verified migration inventory.
  • A Reserve link alone does not establish which dates reach the basket. Checkout and payment have not been tested.
  • The sampled FX6 and studio product titles were H1 headings. Heading issues need checking by template.
  • Existing availability already includes text and quantities; clearer wording needs to connect it to selected dates.
  • A learning hub exists and needs better organization.
  • Sitemap inclusion does not prove search-engine indexing. Possible test or duplicate pages need individual review before removal.
  • Recorded CSS settings support the motion findings but do not prove actual frame drops. Mobile, keyboard and screen-reader behaviour still need direct validation.
  • The original page review used a logged-in browser. Anonymous behaviour may differ.

The earlier WordPress quick-win estimates are not commitments for the replacement platform. I retain the underlying findings because they remain relevant to the new design.

What remains to confirm

I recommend confirming the rental and delivery rules, active rooms and services, and kit contents with Frederik first. Detailed payment, credit, availability and migration checks can then be assigned to the development and operations team if the project proceeds. My possible contribution to visuals, UI mockups or catalogue work remains to be agreed.
