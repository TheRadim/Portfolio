Suggested priorities

24 October 2026

I recommend the order below for discussion. “Essential” means a foundation for a reliable replacement, not a promise that I will implement it. Effort is relative, not a time or cost estimate. Confidence describes the strength of the recommendation, not certainty about the current technical system.

Essential

Recommendation Customer and business benefit Depends on Relative effort Confidence
Preserve availability, rental prices, payments, invoices, accounts and credit Customers can book reliably; SimpleRent protects existing obligations Agreed system owners, business rules, integration and migration checks High High need; implementation unverified
Use five sections and six gear groups with one coherent Rent/Buy catalogue Fewer unnecessary steps; spaces and services are easier to find Active offers and agreed product placement Medium-high High direction; full mapping pending
Add prominent search, relevant filters and consistent sorting Faster equipment selection and better comparisons Complete attributes, aliases and booking history for popularity Medium-high High; ranking needs validation
Make contents, dates, availability and total clear before booking Fewer incomplete setups and mistaken rental periods Verified manifests, prices, per-item periods and planning rules High High need; system support pending
Offer pickup/delivery in the basket with a confirmed total Customers understand fulfilment and its cost Coverage, item eligibility, fees, timing and return arrangements Medium-high Medium; operating rules pending
Provide usable mobile controls, visible focus and readable contrast More customers can complete the core tasks Responsive layouts and direct device/keyboard checks Medium High

Improve next

Recommendation Customer and business benefit Depends on Relative effort Confidence
Recommend compatible extras and relevant kit alternatives Helps complete a setup; introduces useful products without pressure Compatibility links, quantities, kit contents, stock and price comparison Medium-high Medium; suggestion rules need testing
Organize a compact resource library around kits and rental questions More confidence and fewer repeated support questions Accurate guides and someone responsible for updates Medium High direction; content effort pending
Improve upcoming-rental information, saved lists and repeat selection Less repeated work for returning customers Account permissions, retained history and fresh stock/price checks Medium-high Medium; private workflow unreviewed
Refine cards, typography and restrained motion within the existing identity Easier scanning and a more consistent experience Agreed component layouts and accessibility checks Medium High
Add a clear room comparison and accurate floor plan Better space enquiries and fewer suitability misunderstandings Confirmed active rooms, measurements, acoustics and access details Medium Medium

Essential product information and access details do not wait for the later visual refinements. Likewise, existing saved lists or account benefits need a migration decision even if their improved interface comes later.

Explore later

Option Potential benefit What would justify it Relative effort Confidence
Automated room booking Convenient reservations and less manual coordination Reliable calendars, pricing, access and confirmation rules High Medium-low until operations are understood
A full custom kit builder More flexibility for complex shoots Demonstrated demand and dependable component availability High Low until validated
A video library or current courses More practical learning support Useful material and a clear content owner Medium-high Medium-low
New loyalty features beyond existing credit Possible repeat-rental benefit Customer need, commercial value and maintainable rules Medium-high Low until validated

I do not recommend reviving an unfinished podcast or keeping outdated courses simply to fill a section. Existing customer credit remains essential regardless of any later loyalty ideas.

What I propose doing next

I recommend reviewing this order with Frederik first. If visuals would help, I will develop selected UI mockups once we agree which screens matter most. Catalogue mapping, integration, migration and implementation need separate ownership and scope. The interface parts are discussion material, not a finished visual design or working booking system.
