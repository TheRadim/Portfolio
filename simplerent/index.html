Recommendations for SimpleRent

Radim Theiner · 24 October 2026

My recommendations for the new SimpleRent website: a clearer structure, easier search and booking, and a proper home for kits, studios and help. Start with the full recommendations; the supporting documents hold the detail.

Read the full recommendationsSupporting materials

What to do now

  1. Go through the recommendations together. I suggest we meet in person at the end of November or in early December.
  2. Answer a few open questions. Which studios, crew roles and kits are active offers, the advance-booking rule, delivery coverage and charges, and who owns product information and platform decisions.
  3. Decide whether I can be more helpful. For example with mockups of selected screens, or with checking the catalogue against the new structure.
  4. Agree where to start.

The recommendations in short

Nine recommendations, one or two sentences each. Every one links to the full text.

  1. 1

    Make the homepage useful immediately

    Keep the real production photography and add a short offer and location line, a prominent search and Browse equipment. People browse without dates and are asked for them when they add gear. One interruption at a time.

  3. 3

    Make equipment easier to find and compare

    Search that knows model names, filters that fit each gear group, one sort menu, removable filter labels, and choices kept in the page address so results can be shared.

    Read in full
  4. 4

    Bring the booking information together

    Dates, quantity, availability, total and the main action in one place. Contents, specifications and compatible extras clearly separated. Pickup or delivery chosen in the basket.

    Read in full
  5. 5

    Explain how renting works before customers ask

    Three short steps first, then pickup and return, payments, insurance and damage as sections that can be linked from any booking decision.

    Read in full
  6. 6

    Give spaces, services and resources a proper home

    Studios with real facts and a floor plan, crew and post-production with a clear enquiry route, and around nine complete guides.

    Read in full
  7. 7

    Refine the visual style and interaction

    Keep the black, white, purple and lime identity. Lime only where it has contrast, one readable typeface, visible keyboard focus, comfortable tap targets and minimal motion.

    Read in full
  8. 8

    Preserve the business behind the website

    Availability, rental pricing, payments, invoices, business permissions and credit must carry over, whatever the new platform is.

    Read in full
  9. 9

    What I propose next

    Discuss the direction first. If it holds, I can develop selected screens, such as the homepage, a filtered listing, a product page or the basket.

    Read in full
