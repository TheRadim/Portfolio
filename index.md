## Background

Radim builds ideas into products and works across strategy, technology, and execution. His experience spans global corporations, early-stage startups, and scale-ups across B2C, B2B, and B2G. He studied International Marketing & Sales in Denmark, has roots in IT and photography, and works as a Project Manager / Product Owner.
